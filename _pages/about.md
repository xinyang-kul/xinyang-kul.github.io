As a Postdoctoral Researcher at [the Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/lr), I work on the PolyMIND project, focusing on AI-enabled multisensor data fusion for polymer bearing cage health monitoring. In collaboration with DPI, SKF, and TKI GCC, I develop online non-destructive testing and intelligent monitoring methods for rotating machinery, with the aim of improving system reliability and predictive maintenance.

I obtained my PhD from the KU Leuven [Mecha(tro)nic System Dynamics (LMSD) group](https://www.mech.kuleuven.be/en/mod), where my research focused on multisensor and multimodal data fusion for structural health monitoring. My research interests span AI-driven structural health monitoring, non-destructive testing, multisensor data fusion, and intelligent diagnostics and prognostics. With additional experience in embedded edge AI and Texas Instruments hardware acceleration, I aim to bridge advanced AI methodologies with real-world engineering systems for reliable and deployable health monitoring. Before this, I graduated with a Master's degree from the School of Automotive Studies at [Tongji University](https://autoen.tongji.edu.cn/) in 2022. I received my Bachelor's degree in Vehicle Engineering, with the honor of Excellent Graduate, from [Chongqing University](https://mve.cqu.edu.cn/jxyyzgcywb/Home.htm) in 2019.

If you’re interested in collaborating, please feel free to contact me and I’d be happy to discuss potential opportunities!
