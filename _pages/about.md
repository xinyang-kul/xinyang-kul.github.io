As a Postdoctoral Researcher at [the Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/lr), I work on the PolyMIND project, focusing on AI-enabled multisensor data fusion for polymer bearing cage health monitoring. In collaboration with DPI, SKF, and TKI GCC, I develop online non-destructive testing and intelligent monitoring methods for rotating machinery, with the aim of improving system reliability and predictive maintenance.

I obtained my Ph.D. from the [Mecha(tro)nic System Dynamics (LMSD) group at KU Leuven](https://www.mech.kuleuven.be/en/mod), where my research focused on multisensor and multimodal data fusion for structural health monitoring. My research interests span <strong> AI-driven structural health monitoring </strong>, <strong>non-destructive testing</strong>, <strong> multisensor data fusion</strong>, and <strong> intelligent diagnostics and prognostics </strong>. With additional experience in embedded edge AI and Texas Instruments hardware acceleration, I aim to bridge advanced AI methodologies with real-world engineering systems for reliable and deployable health monitoring solutions. 

I am always open to research collaborations and discussions on AI-enabled structural health monitoring, non-destructive testing, and intelligent engineering systems. Please feel free to get in touch if you are interested in exploring potential collaborations!
